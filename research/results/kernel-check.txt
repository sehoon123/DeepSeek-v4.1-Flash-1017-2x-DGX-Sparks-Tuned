The bug 0039 fixes: a row holding a NaN makes keys 0 (a NaN has no key); with fewer than `count` keys left (a row of
NaN: the usual shape of a numerical fault) the merge kernel read logits at id 0xffffffff, 16 GiB past the row. Where
that address is unmapped it is an illegal address, a context-wide sticky error that ends every stream of the
engine; where it is mapped, a stray read whose value the host ignored (it drew a NaN row from the whole row).
0039: no read at a key 0; a row holding a NaN ends its own stream with an error (core Stream.fault; the other
streams' draws go on), and the prompt's kept states are dropped; a lost CUDA context exits the process (status 70)
for the service to restart it.
